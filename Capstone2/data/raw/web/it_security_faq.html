IT Security FAQ

Last updated 1 September 2025 by the Information Security team.

Passwords and multi-factor authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters. You do not need to change your password on a schedule, only when you suspect it has been exposed. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN, the HR portal and all finance systems.

Can I use email on my personal phone?

Yes, through the Outlook app only, enrolled in the company's mobile device management (Intune). MFA is mandatory on personal phones and the app enforces a 6-digit PIN. Native mail apps are blocked.

Phishing

If you clicked a link or opened an attachment in a suspicious email, disconnect from the network, do not enter any more credentials and call the IT Service Desk on extension 4444 immediately. Then use the Report Phish button in Outlook. Reporting quickly matters more than embarrassment: there is no penalty for reporting a genuine mistake.

Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost laptop or phone to the IT Service Desk within one hour. The device will be remotely locked and wiped. A police reference number is required for stolen devices.

USB drives and file sharing

USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. Share files internally through SharePoint and externally only through the approved secure file transfer service. Personal cloud storage (e.g. personal Dropbox or Google Drive) must not be used for company data.

Travelling abroad with a laptop

Inform Information Security before taking a company laptop to a country outside the UK, EU or India. A loaner laptop may be issued for high-risk destinations.
